# Past mine rescue contest problems

Source PDFs for building and solving practice problems. They are kept outside `public/` so the app build does not ship them.

## `msha-archive/`

The old MSHA contest problem archive (`arlweb.msha.gov/minerescue/contest/problems/`), which is no longer on the live MSHA site. Every file was recovered from the Internet Archive's Wayback Machine. `sources.json` lists each file's original URL and the Wayback capture it came from.

- `coal/<year>/`: coal mine rescue contest problems and judge books, 2004 to 2018, plus some first aid, preshift and results sheets.
- `coal/international/`: an international contest problem.
- `metal/`: a few metal/nonmetal problems from 1998 to 2000.
- `BenchResources/`: bench contest references (BG-4, BioPak 240-R and 240-S).

Not recovered: the 1999 coal problems (not archived as PDFs) and the 2003, 2005 and 2009 National problems, which MSHA posted only as self-extracting `.exe` files.

## `virginia-energy/2026/`

Every 2026 contest PDF linked from Virginia Energy's past contests page (`energy.virginia.gov/coal/coal-mine-safety/PastContests.shtml`), downloaded October 2026. Files keep their original names under `images/MRT/2026/`.

- `Tennessee/`: Day 1 and Day 2 problems and judge packets, the preshift judges book, and the 2026 Nationals Day 1 and Day 2 problems and written exams.
- `MorgantownWV/`, `Ohio/`, `Virginia/`, `Utah/`, `Indiana/`: regional contest problems, packets, written exams and results.
- `WVU_BenchBash/`: bench contest problems (BG4, BioPak 240, Pro Air).
